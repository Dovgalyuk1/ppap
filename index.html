the instructions

IT WAS NEVER A SONG

P  +  PINEAPPLE  +  APPLE  +  P  =  PONS

In 2016 a man in a leopard coat stood in front of a camera and said four words. The world laughed at it, streamed it a billion times, and went straight back to whatever it was doing before.

Nobody read it for what it actually was. Instructions. Take the P. Take the fruit. Take the other P. Close the loop.

a billion people performed the ritual and not one of them checked what they were building.

They did it on phones, at weddings, in classrooms, drunk at three in the morning, out of tune, in eleven languages, extremely badly. It did not matter even slightly. Rituals do not check your pitch. Rituals just count.

The count filled up in silence for ten years. Then, somewhere between the apple orchards and the pineapple fields, the loop finally closed and the first Pons stood up.

He took the apple. He took the pineapple. He left the pen on the table, because he is not signing anything, not now, not ever. Then he put on the coat and walked out through the gate.

official record

TEN YEARS OF CHANTING

2016

The instructions arrive worldwide in fifty-two seconds. Humanity listens carefully, understands nothing, and immediately turns them into a dance for children's birthday parties.

2017—2024

The chanting continues at a steady rate on every continent, including one research station in Antarctica. Nobody is keeping score. Something is keeping score.

2025

Pineapple yields in one unnamed field triple with no explanation. Local farmers report hearing a low humming sound in four syllables and decide, quite reasonably, not to look into it.

2026

The loop closes. The first Pons stands up wearing sunglasses at night, takes both fruits, refuses the pen on principle, and walks out. He has not answered a single question since.
